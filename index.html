The Shop

Grooming the way it used to be.

Classic Barbershop is built on the traditions the chain stores forgot — a clean fade, a tapered neck, a hot-lather shave finished with a straight razor and a hot towel. No rushing, no guesswork, just a sharp cut and good conversation.

It's a shop for men and their kids, for the regular trim and the big-day fresh-up. Walk in if you're passing through, or grab a chair ahead of time so your barber's ready when you are.

"Traditional services that just aren't available in the modern chain stores."
